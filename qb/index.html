Integrations

QuickBooks Online

A read-only connection between Mattra Inc. QuickBooks Online and our reporting, used to measure which marketing actually produces invoiced work.

What this connection can do

  • Read customers, invoices, estimates and payments.
  • Nothing is ever written back. It cannot create, change or delete anything in QuickBooks.
  • Figures are used in the Mattra marketing report so lead sources are measured against revenue, not form fills.

Who can see it

Access is limited to Mattra Inc. and Web Education Services, the marketing partner that prepares the reporting. The data is never sold, and it is not shared with advertising platforms.
