Connect QuickBooks

Authorize read-only access to the Mattra Inc. QuickBooks Online company.

Not live yet. The Intuit application is still being registered, so this page exists so the connect address resolves. It will start the authorization once production keys are issued.

What you will be asked to approve

Intuit will ask you to choose the Mattra Inc. company and grant accounting access. Approving it allows customers, invoices, estimates and payments to be read for reporting. It grants no ability to change anything in QuickBooks.

Revoking access

You can disconnect at any time from inside QuickBooks under Settings, then Apps. Access stops immediately.
