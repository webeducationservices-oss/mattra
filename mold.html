Why Mold Happens in Maine Homes

Maine’s climate creates ideal conditions for mold growth.

Long winters and freeze-thaw cycles create condensation inside attics and walls. Ice dams trap moisture. Basements and crawl spaces retain humidity. Poor ventilation and insulation gaps allow moisture to linger.

Most companies focus on mold removal.

We focus on why it happened.
