Rebate Tiers

How Income Tiers Affect Your Rebate

Baseline

Any Income

40%
Up to $4,000 Back

Every Maine homeowner who installs qualifying insulation through a certified contractor is eligible for 40% of their project cost back, up to $4,000. No income verification required. This is the baseline rebate that applies to all qualifying projects.

Moderate Income

Moderate Income

60%
Up to $6,000 Back

Homeowners who meet the Efficiency Maine moderate income threshold qualify for 60% back on their project cost, up to $6,000. Income verification is required. Contact Efficiency Maine directly or ask us during your assessment — we can help determine whether you are likely to qualify.

HEAP / SNAP

Low Income

80%
Up to $8,000 Back

Homeowners who participate in HEAP (Home Energy Assistance Program) or SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program) qualify for 80% of project costs back, up to $8,000. This is the most generous tier and can cover the large majority of a full home insulation project for eligible households.
