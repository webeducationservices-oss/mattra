Know Your Numbers Before You Commit

Most homeowners go into a contractor meeting without any idea what a project costs — which makes it hard to evaluate what you’re being quoted. These calculators give you a realistic ballpark before anyone shows up at your door.

They’re built for Maine — using local installation cost ranges, current Efficiency Maine rebate percentages, and realistic project scopes for the kinds of homes we work on every day. The estimates are not quotes, but they’ll tell you whether a project is in your budget and how much of it rebates and financing can cover.

After you run the numbers, the next step is a free in-home assessment. We measure everything, confirm your rebate eligibility, and give you a firm price before any work begins.

Efficiency Maine Rebate Calculator

Find out how much Efficiency Maine will rebate on your insulation project. Select your project areas (attic, wall, rim joist, basement, crawl space, air sealing), enter square footage, and choose your income tier. Shows estimated project cost, rebate amount (40%–80% depending on income tier, up to $8,000), and estimated out-of-pocket cost in real time.

Attic Insulation Cost Estimator

Estimate the cost of adding or upgrading attic insulation in a Maine home. Enter your attic square footage, current R-value (if known), target R-value (default R-49 per Maine code), and material preference. See your estimated project cost range, estimated rebate, and estimated out-of-pocket after Efficiency Maine rebate.

Spray Foam Project Estimator

Estimate the cost of a spray foam insulation project. Select your application type (rim joist, basement walls, unvented attic, wall cavities), enter the area in square footage, and choose your foam type. See estimated project cost range based on typical Maine spray foam pricing, and applicable Efficiency Maine rebate if the area qualifies.

Basement & Crawl Space Cost Estimator

Estimate the cost of a basement insulation or crawl space encapsulation project. Select your project type (rim joist only, basement wall insulation, full crawl space encapsulation, or combination), enter the area in square footage or linear perimeter. See estimated cost range, estimated Efficiency Maine rebate, and estimated out-of-pocket.
