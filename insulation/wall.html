Wall Insulation Maine: Dense Pack Cellulose & Retrofit Installation

You Don’t Have to Gut Your Walls to Insulate Them

The biggest misconception about wall insulation retrofits is that you need to tear out all your drywall to do it. In most cases, you do not. Dense pack cellulose can be blown into existing wall cavities through small holes drilled from the exterior or interior — typically covered by the existing siding or patched and painted when the work is complete.

This makes wall insulation one of the most accessible upgrades for older Maine homes. If your walls are uninsulated or underinsulated — common in homes built before the 1980s — adding wall insulation significantly improves comfort, reduces drafts from outlets and switch plates, and lowers heating costs month after month.

Wall insulation projects qualify for Efficiency Maine rebates when installed by a certified contractor. We handle the documentation and application support as part of every project.

Two Methods

Two Methods for Insulating Walls Without Full Tear-Out

Dense Pack Cellulose

The most common wall retrofit method in Maine. Cellulose is blown under pressure through small holes, compressing to approximately 3.5 lbs/cu ft. It fills the cavity completely, eliminates air pockets, and reduces convective heat loss. R-3.7 to R-4.0 per inch. Best for wood-framed walls with standard stud bays.

Spray Foam for Walls

Best for new construction, walls open during renovation, or rim joist areas. Spray foam is fully air-sealed and moisture-resistant. Closed-cell spray foam adds structural rigidity and acts as a vapor barrier. Higher cost but the right choice when walls are already open.

Common Scenarios

Wall Insulation Scenarios We Handle

Existing Walls, No Drywall Removal

  • Drill 2” holes in each stud bay
  • Access from exterior through siding or interior
  • Dense pack cellulose blown into cavities
  • Holes plugged and patched
  • Siding restored to original condition

Existing Walls, No Drywall Removal

We drill 2-inch holes in each stud bay from the exterior through the siding or from the interior, dense pack the cavities, then plug and patch. Siding is restored to its original condition. Most homeowners can’t tell work was done.

Walls Open During Renovation

  • Install batts, dense pack, or spray foam
  • Combine with air sealing for maximum performance
  • Fraction of the cost compared to retrofitting later
  • Full access to stud bays and cavities
  • Ideal time for comprehensive insulation

Walls Open During Renovation

If your walls are already open during a renovation, this is the ideal time to insulate. We can install batts, dense pack, or spray foam and combine it with air sealing. Doing it now costs a fraction of what it would cost to retrofit later.

Older & Balloon-Frame Homes

  • Pre-1940s construction with continuous wall cavities
  • Cavities run from foundation to roofline
  • Special attention to full cavity fill
  • No gaps or voids in insulation
  • Experience with non-standard framing

Older & Balloon-Frame Homes

In pre-1940s balloon-frame homes, wall cavities can run continuously from the foundation to the roofline. These require special attention to ensure a full cavity fill without gaps. Our team has extensive experience with older Maine housing stock and non-standard framing.

Our Process

How Our Wall Insulation Process Works

1

Wall Assessment

We assess your wall construction, stud bay locations, existing insulation levels, and the best access method. We note any moisture issues, pest damage, or non-standard framing that could affect the installation.

2

Access Drilling

Small holes are drilled in each stud bay. Exterior access through siding is preferred to minimize interior disruption, but interior drilling is used when necessary. Every effort is made to minimize visual impact.

3

Dense Pack Installation

Cellulose is blown under controlled pressure to achieve proper fill density. Each stud bay is monitored during installation and verified for complete fill to ensure consistent coverage throughout the wall assembly.

4

Patching, Cleanup & Documentation

All access holes are plugged and patched. Siding or interior surfaces are restored to their original condition. We provide an R-value certificate and handle all Efficiency Maine rebate documentation.
