What We Do

What a Building Inspection Helps You Understand Before You Move Forward

A strong building inspection gives you more than a list of defects. It helps you understand how the property is performing, where visible concerns may exist, and what questions deserve a closer look before you make a decision.

A property condition inspection can help uncover:

  • Visible wear and deferred maintenance
  • Moisture-related warning signs
  • Attic, roof, basement, and crawl space concerns
  • Potential repair priorities
  • Items that may affect buyer confidence or negotiation strategy

For Maine home buyers, that due diligence matters before you commit fully. For Maine home sellers, it can help you understand what could become a conversation point during the transaction. For both sides, inspection findings and next steps matter just as much as the findings themselves.
