Removal and remediation are not the same thing

Mold removal treats the growth you can see. Mold remediation is a complete process: containment, safe removal of contaminated materials, antimicrobial treatment, correcting the moisture source, and verifying the area is clear.

The difference matters because mold always has a moisture source. If that source is still there, cleaning the surface only buys time. Remediation deals with why the mold was there, not just what was visible.

How the job goes

Every job follows the same order, so nothing gets cleaned up only to come back.

  1. Find the source.We inspect the area and track down the moisture that is feeding the mold.
  2. Contain the area.Barriers and negative air pressure keep spores out of the rest of the house while we work.
  3. Remove what cannot be saved.Contaminated materials are taken out safely.
  4. Treat the surfaces.Affected areas get an antimicrobial treatment.
  5. Fix the cause.We correct the moisture source, whether that is a leak, poor ventilation, or drainage.
  6. Verify and document.We confirm the area is clear and give you written documentation for your records or your insurance claim.

Common questions

How much does mold remediation cost?

It depends on the size of the affected area, the type of mold, and whether structural materials have to be removed. We give you a clear, itemized breakdown before any work begins, so you know the scope and the price first.

How long does mold remediation take?

Most residential remediations take 1 to 5 days depending on scope and complexity. Projects that involve significant demolition and rebuild can take longer. You get a realistic timeline after the first assessment.

Can I stay in my home during the work?

It depends on where the mold is and how much work is involved. Containment barriers and negative air pressure protect the rest of the house. If the work is extensive, or someone in the home is very young, elderly, or immunocompromised, temporary relocation may be the better choice, and we will tell you plainly.

Does homeowners insurance cover mold remediation?

Coverage depends on the cause. Mold from a sudden covered event, such as a burst pipe, may be covered. Mold from a long-running moisture problem usually is not. We can provide written documentation of the findings and scope to support a claim.
