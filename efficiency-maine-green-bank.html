Efficiency Maine Green Bank

The Efficiency Maine Trust brings its financing programs together under one name: the Efficiency Maine Green Bank. Here is what is in it, which parts are for homeowners, and where Mattra fits.

What the Green Bank is

The Efficiency Maine Green Bank is a suite of financing offerings for energy projects, run by the Efficiency Maine Trust. Where possible, its programs are designed to bring private capital into gaps in the market for energy efficiency and clean energy equipment and services. They sit alongside the Trust's rebates and incentives for residents and businesses, and they aim at the same goals: saving energy, transforming the market, reducing environmental impact, keeping things fair, and leveraging the private sector.

This is a representative selection of the programs. It is not the full list of every current, past, or planned initiative.

For homeowners

Home Energy Loans

Loans for residential energy upgrades: insulation, air sealing, and heat pumps. Four options from 0% to 7.99% APR, up to $25,000, unsecured. Low- and moderate-income households can qualify for a lower income-based rate. Mattra is a Registered Vendor, which is required to use these loans.

Mobile home owners

Mobile Home Initiative Loans

Loans for people taking part in the Mobile Home Initiative, which helps owners of mobile homes switch to whole-home heat pump systems. If your home does not qualify for the heat pump because of heat loss or underbelly damage, insulation rebates of up to $5,600 may come first.

Questions people ask

What is the Efficiency Maine Green Bank?

It is the umbrella name for the financing offerings run by the Efficiency Maine Trust. These loans and financing tools complement the Trust's rebates and other incentives, and are designed to bring private capital into places where the market is not meeting the need for energy efficiency and clean energy equipment.

Is the Green Bank the same thing as the Efficiency Maine rebate?

No. Rebates are a payment toward a project and follow their own rules, including a lifetime limit and technical requirements. The Green Bank lends money to help you pay for the project up front. You can use a loan with or without a rebate.

Which Green Bank program is for homeowners?

Home Energy Loans, which fund insulation, air sealing, and heat pumps at your home. Owners of mobile homes who take part in the Mobile Home Initiative may also use Mobile Home Initiative Loans. The other programs serve businesses, municipalities, and care facilities.

Does Mattra offer Green Bank loans?

The loans come from Efficiency Maine, not from Mattra. Mattra is an Efficiency Maine Residential Registered Vendor, which is the requirement for using Home Energy Loans, and we help you scope the project and coordinate the paperwork.
