Energy Loss

Why Home Insulation Matters in Maine

Homes across Maine lose a large amount of heat through poorly insulated attics, walls, basements, and crawl spaces. Without proper insulation, warm air escapes during the winter while cold air enters your living space.

Common signs your home may need insulation upgrades:

  • Drafty rooms during the winter
  • High heating bills
  • Uneven temperatures between floors
  • Ice dams forming on the roof
  • Cold floors above basements or crawl spaces

When insulation is installed correctly and combined with air sealing improvements, homeowners can dramatically reduce heat loss.

Rebates & Incentives

Efficiency Maine Insulation Rebates

Many Maine homeowners qualify for rebates through the Efficiency Maine Home Energy Savings Program, which helps offset the cost of insulation upgrades.

Available incentives may include:

  • Income-eligible rebates up to $8,000
  • Any-income rebates up to $4,000 for insulation improvements
  • Air sealing rebates up to $500
  • Additional assistance for HEAP, SNAP, TANF, or MaineCare households
