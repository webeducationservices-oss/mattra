Website Migration Strategy and Goals

Web Education Services Final Page Hierarchy and Phase 1 Launch Plan Prepared for Justin Babcock | May 13, 2026 Purpose: Create a clean, controlled migration plan for moving Web Education Services away from Duda into a new CMS without carrying the old site mess into the new system. This plan defines what launches in Phase 1, what comes back in Phase 1.5, and what should be redirected, archived, noindexed, or reviewed later. Recommendation: launch lean with 13 core pages, then add back the best resources, workshops, guides, and blog content in controlled batches. Do not rewrite every current page before launch.

Migration Decision Framework

  • Rewrite and launch pages that explain the business, generate leads, or support trust.
  • Consolidate overlapping pages into stronger parent pages instead of recreating every old URL.
  • Migrate educational assets into a Resources structure after the core site is clear.
  • Noindex thank-you pages, class pages, duplicate pages, and utility pages that should not appear in search.
  • Purge true junk, placeholders, and pages with no active purpose.
  • Create redirects from old URLs to the best new destination before launch.

Phase 1 Launch Structure

These pages should be written, loaded into the new CMS, QA tested, and launched first.

  • Home / Primary brand message, main service paths, lead generation.
  • Websites /websites Website builds, hosting, maintenance, and AI-supported updates.
  • AI Website Editor /ai-website-editor My AI Editor marketing page and demo/request access path.
  • Marketing Launchpad /marketing-launchpad Training programs, course options, and guided marketing support.
  • Consulting /consulting Marketing systems, AI implementation, automation, and project help.
  • Pricing /pricing One consistent pricing page for all public offers.
  • Resources /resources Hub for guides, workshops, checklists, templates, and tutorials.
  • Office Hours /office-hours Clear weekly support/workshop landing page.
  • Support /support Client support path and help requests.
  • About /about Consolidated story, trust, credentials, and why WES exists.
  • Contact /contact One primary contact and booking page.
  • Privacy Policy /privacy-policy Legal page.
  • Terms of Service /terms-of-service Legal page.

Recommended Main Navigation

  • Websites /websites Primary service and revenue page.
  • AI Website Editor /ai-website-editor Product/service page for My AI Editor.
  • Marketing Launchpad /marketing-launchpad Training, course, and guided marketing page.
  • Consulting /consulting Marketing systems, AI, and automation help.
  • Pricing /pricing One consistent pricing page.
  • Resources /resources Guides, workshops, checklists, blog, and tutorials.
  • Contact /contact Primary conversion page.

CMS Content Organization

The new CMS should prevent the old page sprawl from coming back. Content should live inside clear collections, not random standalone pages.

  • Services Used for Websites, AI Website Editor, Marketing Launchpad, Consulting
  • Resources Used for Guides, checklists, templates, workshops
  • Blog Posts Used for AI tutorials, tool reviews, marketing articles
  • Workshops Used for Office hours and training sessions
  • Case Studies Used for Client results and proof
  • FAQs Used for Reusable FAQ blocks across pages
  • Testimonials Used for Proof used across pages
  • Utility Pages Used for Thank-you pages and hidden pages

Launch Timeline and Execution

Goal: launch a clean, conversion-ready site without waiting for every legacy page to be rewritten.

4-Week Launch Schedule

  • Week 1: Strategy, Sitemap, and CMS Setup Finalize the page hierarchy, redirect map, CMS collections, templates, navigation, footer, and page ownership.
  • Week 2: Core Page Writing Write the 13 Phase 1 launch pages. Focus on clear offers, strong CTAs, consistent pricing, internal links, and SEO metadata.
  • Week 3: Build, Load, Redirect, and QA Load the core pages into the new CMS, build templates, add tracking, prepare redirects, create sitemap, test forms, and review mobile layouts.
  • Week 4: Launch and Stabilize Move DNS, submit sitemap, test redirects, confirm GA4 events, monitor Search Console, fix launch issues, and publish the first resource batch if ready.

Phase 1 Writing Priority Order

  1. Home Explain the business in plain language: websites, marketing education, AI tools, and support.
  2. Websites Sell website builds, hosting, maintenance, and AI-supported updates.
  3. AI Website Editor Explain My AI Editor, how it works, who it is for, preview/publish, and request access.
  4. Marketing Launchpad Consolidate the training offers into one clear decision page.
  5. Pricing Fix all pricing confusion with one transparent pricing page.
  6. Consulting Position consulting around AI, marketing systems, automation, and strategy.
  7. Resources Create the hub that lets older content come back cleanly.
  8. Office Hours Rewrite sign-up as a helpful landing page with topics, value, and form.
  9. About Tell the WES story with credibility, client experience, and real differentiation.
  10. Contact Simplify all contact paths into one page.
  11. Support Keep existing clients from getting lost during migration.
  12. Privacy and Terms Legal pages.

Content Expansion Strategy

Goal: rebuild content depth after launch without recreating the old mess. Phase 1.5 begins immediately after launch and runs for 4 weeks.

Batch 1: Core Resource Guides

Email Marketing Guide, Blogging Guide, On-Page SEO Guide, Digital Advertising Guide, Premium Content Guide Support the educational positioning and create strong internal links to Launchpad and Consulting.

Batch 2: Local and Reputation Resources

Local SEO Guide, Reputation Management Guide, Google Business listing content, review response content Support local service search intent and consulting conversations.

Batch 3: Social and Checklist Consolidation

Social Media Guide, Checklists Hub, Website Checklist, Facebook Ad Guide, Email Checklist Turn scattered how-to content into organized, useful resources.

Batch 4: Workshops and Proof

Workshops Hub, Case Studies, FAQ, Testimonials Add trust, proof, and recurring engagement paths.

Launch Checklist and Final Direction

  • All Phase 1 pages written, edited, and approved.
  • All old URLs assigned to redirect, noindex, archive, or migrate status.
  • 301 redirects loaded and tested before DNS move.
  • GA4 and Google Tag Manager events tested for forms, phone clicks, email clicks, pricing views, and demo requests.
  • Sitemap generated from new CMS and submitted after launch.
  • Robots.txt reviewed to ensure public pages are crawlable and hidden pages are blocked or noindexed correctly.
  • Forms tested on desktop and mobile.
  • Mobile layout reviewed for every launch page.
  • Meta titles and descriptions added to every launch page.
  • Internal links added from every core page to at least two related pages.
  • Post-launch crawl errors and 404s reviewed daily during the first week.

The Phase 1 goal is not to move every page out of Duda. The Phase 1 goal is to launch a clear site that explains the business, creates conversion paths, and gives the new CMS a clean structure. The old content should come back only when it has a defined role in the new hierarchy. Recommended launch size: 13 core pages plus optional first resource batch. Recommended Phase 1.5 expansion: 12 to 18 supporting pages over the next 4 weeks. Everything else should be redirected, archived, noindexed, or reviewed later.
