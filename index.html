The Problem Most Contractors Miss

Most Contractors Treat Symptoms

They remove mold. They add insulation. They repair rot. But they rarely diagnose why it happened.

Moisture, air leakage, insulation gaps, ventilation failures, and structural damage are connected systems. Fix one piece in isolation and the problem usually comes back — in the same spot, or somewhere new.

We find the root cause, correct it, and verify the fix. That's the difference between solving it and just treating it.

Efficiency Maine Registered Vendor

Rebates That Actually Reduce What You Pay

Efficiency Maine helps residents upgrade insulation, air sealing, and home performance — and covers a meaningful portion of the bill for most eligible work.

Rebates are income-tiered. Most households qualify for 40% to 80% of the project cost, with dollar caps of $4,000, $6,000, or $8,000 depending on income. Funding is limited.

As a registered vendor, we:

  • Identify which upgrades qualify for your situation
  • Estimate rebate amounts against real project scope
  • Complete work to program standards
  • Handle the documentation
