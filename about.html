Why Mattra Exists

Mattra Started Because the Trades Don't Talk to Each Other

Over 30 years ago, our founder was working with property developers and home builders across Maine, managing entire job sites — excavation, framing, insulation, finish work, the whole sequence. From that vantage point, the same pattern showed up over and over: every trade did its own piece well, and somehow the finished home still failed.

Foundations drained badly. Air leaked where it shouldn't. Insulation went in around moisture problems nobody had checked for. Each trade walked away clean. The homeowner inherited the gaps between them.

That's where Mattra began — as a single team that managed all trades on a job site so the communication failures between them stopped costing homeowners their comfort, their energy bills, and their structures.

Over the years, that approach evolved. We brought in building science certifications. We saw — earlier than most — how moisture, air movement, insulation, and structural decay are one connected system, not four. We added remediation, weatherization, and consulting because the homes we were called into kept needing all of it together.

We're still the same kind of company we started as: one team that sees the whole picture, instead of a chain of subcontractors each holding one piece.

What We Believe

Diagnose Before You Prescribe

Most contractors quote before they understand. The pattern is so common that homeowners stop noticing it: someone walks the property, looks at the visible damage, and within minutes is telling you what the fix costs. The diagnosis step gets skipped because skipping it is faster, and faster wins more jobs.

We don't operate that way — and not because we're slower. Because anyone who quotes before diagnosing is guessing. And when contractors guess, homeowners pay for the guess.

A free expert inspection is how we begin every job. Our expert inspector walks the property, listens to what you've already been told, looks at the connections others missed, and explains what we're seeing — in plain language, before anyone talks about price. If the situation calls for deeper diagnostics like blower door testing or thermal imaging, we'll explain why and what that involves. If it doesn't, we won't sell you what you don't need.

This isn't a methodology we invented. It's the way every other diagnostic profession already works. Doctors don't write prescriptions before they run tests. We don't write quotes before we understand the home.

The Experience

What It's Like to Have Mattra Look at Your Home

Our expert inspector arrives on time, in a marked truck, wearing the actual gear of a working contractor — not a sales uniform. They'll walk the property with you, ask what you've already been told, and look at the parts of your home most people never check: the basement, the attic, the rim joists, the soffits, the spots where two systems meet and where most problems start.
