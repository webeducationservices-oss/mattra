When Mold and Insulation Work Go Together, Financing Can Cover Both

For homeowners dealing with mold caused by insulation gaps, air leakage, or moisture intrusion, the fix usually requires both remediation and weatherization. This is where the Efficiency Maine Green Bank loan does something most homeowners don't realize is possible.

When mold remediation is required to complete eligible insulation or air sealing work, those mold costs can be financed through the same Green Bank loan as "Other Project Costs" — up to 25% of the total financed amount. Most companies that only do mold can't offer this. Most companies that only do insulation can't address the mold. Mattra Inc. handles both, which means the entire project can move forward under one financing umbrella.

This pathway only applies when the mold remediation is tied to a qualifying weatherization project. It's not a rebate, and it doesn't apply to mold-only jobs. But for the homeowner whose mold issue is rooted in air movement, moisture, and insulation problems, it's often the difference between fixing the symptom and fixing the cause.

Common Questions About Efficiency Maine Rebates and Financing

Starting October 1, 2026, Efficiency Maine pays a set amount for each area of the home you insulate rather than a percentage of project cost. Each area — attic, wall, and basement (or mobile home underbelly) — earns $1,000 to $4,000 depending on how much space is insulated there and your income tier, and a mobile home underbelly earns up to $5,000. Air sealing earns its own separate rebate. Lifetime totals are capped at $8,600 per home, or $5,600 per mobile home. Mattra Inc. is a registered Efficiency Maine vendor and handles the application process for you.

Every Maine homeowner qualifies at the Any Income level with no income verification. The Moderate Income tier (Adjusted Gross Income up to $70,000 for individual filers, or $100,000 for joint filers) and the Low Income tier (HEAP, SNAP, TANF, or MaineCare participants) earn higher amounts for each area insulated. Both require verification. The project must be completed by a registered Efficiency Maine vendor and include before-and-after blower door testing to document the improvement.

In addition to Efficiency Maine rebates, financing is available through the Efficiency Maine Green Bank (0%–7.99% APR, up to $25,000) and the National Energy Improvement Fund (NEIF EnergyPlus loans up to $35,000). The Green Bank loan covers insulation, air sealing, and up to 25% of project cost for related health and safety work like mold remediation. NEIF covers a broader scope including windows, doors, and roofing tied to the project. Mattra Inc. is registered for both.

Processing times vary, but most rebates are issued within four to eight weeks after the completed application and documentation are submitted. Mattra Inc. submits all documentation promptly after project completion.
