How Much Are Efficiency Maine Insulation Rebates?

Efficiency Maine offers three rebate tiers based on income. Higher rebate percentages and maximum amounts are available for those who qualify through income verification.

Any Income

40% of project cost
Up to $4,000

No income verification required. Available to all homeowners.

Moderate Income

60% of project cost
Up to $6,000

Income verification required. Expanded rebate access.

Low Income

80% of project cost
Up to $8,000

Qualifies through HEAP, MaineCare, or verified low-income status.

The Energy Upgrade Bundle is a common entry point for Maine homeowners. Mattra helps you understand which tier and rebate package may apply to your home and situation.

Program details and incentive amounts are subject to change. Eligibility depends on project scope, income verification, and current program availability.

Financing Options for Weatherization, Insulation, and Beyond

Rebates can lower part of your cost. Financing can cover the rest. Together, they make energy upgrades much more affordable and achievable now.

Efficiency Maine Green Bank Loans

Interest rates: Fixed 4.99% – 5.99% APR
Loan terms: Up to 12 years
Loan amounts: $5,000 – $50,000

Available for residential energy projects including insulation, air sealing, heat pumps, and weatherization. Works alongside rebates to reduce your out-of-pocket cost.

NEIF EnergyPlus Loans

Loan amounts: Up to $25,000
Fixed rate: Competitive, all-inclusive
Coverage: Insulation, air sealing, HVAC, heat pumps, and more

NEIF (Northeast Energy Efficiency Partnerships) offers flexible financing designed for multi-measure energy projects. Can be combined with Efficiency Maine rebates.

Many homeowners combine Efficiency Maine rebates with Green Bank or NEIF financing. This approach lowers the upfront cost significantly and spreads the remaining balance across a manageable monthly payment.
