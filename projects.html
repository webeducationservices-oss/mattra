Common Questions

Common Questions About Specialty Home Services

A home energy audit typically looks at how your home is performing, where energy may be escaping, and what changes could improve comfort and efficiency. It helps you prioritize what to fix first.

A blower door test is useful when you want to measure air leakage, diagnose draft issues, or understand how tightly your home is sealed before making insulation or air sealing decisions.

Rodent cleanup usually involves removing contaminated material, cleaning affected areas, and addressing the conditions that allowed the problem to build up in the first place.

Insulation should be removed when it is contaminated, wet, damaged, compressed, or no longer doing its job well. Attic insulation removal is also common after rodent activity or moisture problems.

Home drying services help reduce moisture after leaks, damp conditions, or water related problems so the area can stabilize before cleanup, repair, or rebuilding begins.
