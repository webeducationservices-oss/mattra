What We Measure

What a Blower Door Test Measures and Why It Matters

A blower door test is a home air leakage test that helps measure how much outside air is moving through your house. Instead of guessing where heat is escaping, the test gives you a clearer picture of whole-home air leakage and how that affects comfort and efficiency.

This matters because many homeowner complaints start with symptoms, not answers. Drafts, uneven temperatures, cold rooms, and rising energy bills can all point to air leakage, but without testing, it is easy to assume the wrong cause.

Blower door testing in Maine helps turn those vague problems into something more useful:

  • A clearer understanding of how much air the home is leaking
  • Better visibility into hidden heat loss
  • A stronger starting point for air sealing priorities
  • More confidence before making insulation or efficiency upgrades
  • A practical path toward better home performance testing and upgrade planning
Warning Signs

Common Signs Your Home May Be Losing Heat Through Air Leaks

Some homes make air leakage obvious. Others show it through patterns that build over time.

Drafts Around Openings

Air movement near windows, doors, attic hatches, or wall areas often points to leakage.

Uneven Room Temperatures

Some rooms feel colder or harder to heat than the rest of the house.

Cold Floors

Lower surfaces may stay chilly even when the heating system is running normally.

High Heating Bills

Energy costs stay high without one clear explanation.

Ongoing Discomfort

The home never feels quite right even though the system is working.

Hard-to-Solve Efficiency Problems

Comfort and efficiency issues keep showing up without a clear fix.

If these sound familiar, blower door testing is often the smart next step. It helps confirm whether air leakage is a major part of the problem before you invest in upgrades.
