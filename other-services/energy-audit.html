What We Uncover

What a Maine Home Energy Audit Can Reveal About Your Home

A Maine home energy audit helps you understand how your house is performing as a system. Instead of guessing why the home feels drafty, uneven, or expensive to heat and cool, the audit helps uncover where those problems may actually be starting.

A home energy assessment can help reveal:

  • Hidden energy loss
  • Drafty rooms and uneven temperatures
  • Air leakage and insulation issues
  • Areas where comfort and efficiency problems overlap
  • Energy-saving opportunities that may not be obvious at first
  • What parts of the house deserve attention before you spend money on upgrades

For many homeowners, the biggest value is clarity. An audit helps you find where your home is losing energy so you can make smarter decisions about what to fix first.

Why Start Here

Why an Energy Audit Is a Better Starting Point Than Guessing at Upgrades

A lot of homeowners jump straight to replacing insulation, adding equipment, or chasing one obvious symptom. The problem is that comfort and efficiency issues are often connected.

A home may feel cold because of air leakage, not just low insulation. A room may overheat because of attic conditions, not just sun exposure. High energy bills may come from multiple smaller losses working together.

That is why an energy audit is so valuable. It helps you:

  • Avoid spending money in the wrong order
  • Prioritize the upgrades with the biggest impact
  • Understand how insulation, air sealing, and testing work together
  • Build a plan around real home performance, not assumptions

A smarter plan starts with understanding the house before choosing the upgrade.
