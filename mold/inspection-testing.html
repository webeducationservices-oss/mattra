Our Testing Services

Our Mold Testing Services

What We Test

  • Indoor air samples from affected areas
  • Outdoor baseline air samples for comparison
  • Spore count analysis by certified lab
  • Detection of hidden airborne contamination

Air Quality Testing

We collect air samples from inside your home and compare them against outdoor baseline levels. Elevated spore counts confirm airborne contamination — even when you can't see visible growth.

This is the most accurate method for detecting hidden mold problems.

What We Identify

  • Swab and tape-lift surface samples
  • Mold species identification
  • Stachybotrys (black mold) detection
  • Remediation approach guidance

Surface Sampling

When visible growth is present, we take swab or tape-lift samples to identify the mold species. Surface testing confirms what type of mold you're dealing with and helps guide the remediation approach.

Results include identification of common species including Stachybotrys (black mold).

What We Detect

  • Temperature differences behind walls
  • Hidden moisture intrusion
  • Growth behind ceilings and floors
  • Included in every inspection — no add-on fee

Thermal Imaging

Our infrared cameras detect temperature differences behind walls, ceilings, and floors — revealing hidden moisture intrusion and growth that isn't visible to the naked eye.

Thermal imaging is included in every Mattra inspection, not an add-on fee.

What to Expect

What to Expect During Your Mold Inspection

1

Visual Survey

We walk the full property — attic, basement, crawl space, and living areas — identifying visible growth, moisture staining, and conditions that encourage mold development.

2

Air & Surface Sampling

We collect samples in affected and comparison areas to establish baselines and measure contamination levels. Samples are analyzed and results are documented in your written report.

3

Thermal Imaging Scan

Our infrared camera identifies moisture behind surfaces that aren't visible during a standard visual inspection. This step often reveals the source of a mold problem before remediation begins.

4

Written Report & Next Steps

You receive a clear, documented summary of what we found, where it is, and what we recommend. If remediation is the right step, we can provide an estimate the same day.
