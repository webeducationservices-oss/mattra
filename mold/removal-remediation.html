Root Cause

Most Companies Remove Mold. We Fix Why It's Growing.

Mold removal treats the symptom. Mold remediation treats the cause.

When mold grows in a Maine home, it's because moisture found a way in — and stayed. Freeze-thaw cycles, ice dams, poor ventilation, and insulation gaps all create the conditions mold needs to thrive. Remove the mold without fixing the moisture source, and it comes back.

Mattra identifies and corrects the moisture source as part of every remediation. That means attic insulation gaps, basement air leakage, crawl space vapor issues — whatever caused the mold to grow in the first place. We don't just remove it. We stop it.

Our Process

Our Mold Remediation Process

1

Assessment & Scope

We evaluate the full extent of the problem — visible growth, hidden moisture, and air quality — and document findings before any work begins. You'll know exactly what's there and what remediation requires.

2

Containment

We isolate the affected area with physical barriers and negative air pressure to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected parts of the home. HEPA filtration runs continuously throughout the project.

3

Safe Removal

Contaminated materials are removed according to IICRC S520 standards. This includes affected drywall, insulation, and structural materials as needed. We handle all demolition — no need for a separate contractor.

4

Antimicrobial Treatment

All affected surfaces receive EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment to eliminate remaining spores and inhibit regrowth. Treatment is applied to structural elements, subfloors, and framing — not just visible surfaces.

5

Clearance Verification

Post-remediation air quality testing confirms the space meets IICRC standards before we close up. You receive written clearance documentation — required by many lenders and insurers.

Attic Mold Removal

Attics are the most common site for mold in Maine homes. Inadequate ventilation and insulation failures allow moisture to accumulate all winter.

We remediate attic mold and correct the ventilation and insulation conditions that caused it — so it doesn't come back.

Basement Remediation Includes

  • Full mold removal from walls and floors
  • Moisture intrusion source identification
  • Drainage and vapor barrier assessment
  • Waterproofing recommendations

Basement Mold Removal

Basements trap moisture year-round through foundation seepage, condensation, and air infiltration.

We remediate basement mold and identify the moisture intrusion source — whether that's a drainage issue, vapor barrier failure, or air leakage.

Crawl Space Remediation Includes

  • Vapor barrier installation

Crawl Space Mold Removal

Crawl spaces are especially vulnerable due to soil moisture, poor air circulation, and seasonal humidity swings.

We remediate crawl space mold, install vapor barriers, and address long-term moisture control through encapsulation.
