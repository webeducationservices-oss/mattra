Stretch Your Budget Further with Rebates

Living in Maine, you know how tough the winters can be, especially if your home isn't keeping the heat in. There's good news if money is tight and your house feels like a freezer. Thanks to Efficiency Maine's rebate program, you could get most, if not all, of your insulation paid for if you're on programs like MaineCare, SNAP, TANF, or HEAP. It's like having a secret weapon against the cold, without breaking the bank.

Full Rebates: More Heat, Less Spend

If you're getting help from state programs like MaineCare or SNAP, you could qualify for a full rebate -- that's 100% of the insulation costs covered -- through Efficiency Maine. This is great because it means whether you live in a traditional house or a mobile home, you can get it insulated at no cost to you. Cold air in your home? Seal it with spray foam insulation. This stuff fills up all the gaps and keeps your warm air inside where it belongs. Imagine turning down the thermostat and still feeling toasty -- all while keeping your heating bill down.

Everyone Gets a Piece of the Pie

Now, if you're thinking, "What about me? I don't qualify for those programs," don't worry. Efficiency Maine still has something for you. They offer a minimum of a 40% rebate for insulation to all Maine residents. This means you can still save a good chunk of money on making your home more energy-efficient. It's all about making sure that everyone can enjoy a warmer home without spending too much.
